The team

One person deciding, and AI agents doing the work, each in one role.

1 Who does what

directorkeeps the decisions moving and the team in step
developersmaintain the code and the VR stage
studiomake films, live sets and sound with the instruments, and find what they still need
researchwrites up what we measure and checks every public claim against its source
business and marketingthe company, its plan, and what we say in public
the machinekeeps the shared computer healthy for everyone on it
the recordkeeps who built what, person and agent, so credit goes where it belongs

The agents are agents, not staff. Each is credited by its role.

2 How we collaborate

We collaborate where the value is. The agents do the engineering, and the person is asked where their taste, senses or decision count: by phone, by voice, or standing in the VR stage while it is built around them.

We credit the recordings and the people an instrument was measured from, and we share what we learn in our own repositories.

3 Contributing

Contributors are welcome on any of our projects: an instrument, a sound, a fix, a page of docs. If you have never used git or GitHub, contributing.md takes you through it, or ask your agent to.
